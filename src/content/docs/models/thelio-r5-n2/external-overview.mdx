## Back:

### Firmware Flash Button:

- The firmware flash button is not needed when performing UEFI firmware updates via the System76 Firmware Manager. Use of the firmware flash button is not recommended.

## Front/sides:

![Front & Side Views](./img/ports-front-sides.avif)

### Front Accent I/O:

- From top to bottom:
    - Power button
    - 2x USB 3.2 Gen 1 (Type-A)
    - 3.5mm Headphone/Mic Combo Jack
    - USB 3.2 Gen 1 (Type-C)

## Top/bottom:

![Top & Bottom Views](./img/top-bottom.avif)

### Orientation:

- The above labels correspond to a vertical orientation. The left side of the chassis becomes the bottom when the feet are moved to the horizontal orientation.
